About Me

I'm Anmol Manni, a graphic designer and front-end developer based in Surrey, BC. I hold a Graphic Design Certificate and a Digital Marketing Certificate, and I've created brand assets and social content as a Digital Marketing & Design Intern at ILP Overseas and a Social Media Intern at Segel India, then moved into front-end web work at Careerguide.com — where design and code meet.

That mix is my edge: I don't just make visuals that look good, I understand how they perform on a real web page, in a real feed, and in real print. I'm currently completing an MBA at University Canada West.
